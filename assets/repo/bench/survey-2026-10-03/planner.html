Methodology and promotion gate

48 training cases and 48 held-out cases per host. Family assignments precede measurement. At most three tree levels, cost-sensitive splitting, no runtime training or model download. 7 rounds rotate variant order. Native timings are round means; jsdom timings are Mitata round medians. Model evaluation is a separate run using the generated branches.

The promotion threshold is at least 4.8% less geometric mean query time in each host, with no individual evaluated case taking over 15% more time. Synthetic warm all-results queries support only this experiment. Cold start, mutation, callback, XML, first-result, and real-application validation need separate evidence beyond this warm-query report.

Ordered element identity is checked before and after timing. The source runtime is unchanged in this experimental comparison. No GPU or competitor speedup is claimed.
